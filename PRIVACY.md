# Privacy Policy for Blackjack Variants

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

Blackjack Variants does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any
personal information.

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-in, and the game never asks for your name,
  email, or any other personal details.
- **No internet use.** The game plays entirely on your device and does not
  send anything over the network.
- **No tracking or analytics.** There is no advertising, analytics, crash
  reporting, or tracking of any kind inside the game.
- **What stays on your device.** Your saved game, your game options, and your
  play statistics are stored only on your own device so you can pick up where
  you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **No real money.** All chips and winnings are pretend. The game offers no
  purchases and no real-money gambling.

If you install a build through TestFlight or Google Play testing, Apple or
Google may collect crash reports and usage data under their own privacy
policies. This game does not add anything to that.

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/YouSeeImNewHere/UnderwayBlackjack/issues>.
